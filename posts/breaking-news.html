This is what happens when Hannah can’t sleep at 3am…

by Hannah

Warning: The following Op/Ed piece was written by Hannah’s hands very early in the morning.  She refuses to accept any association with those responsible for its content and blames too many political museums, a propensity for silliness, and the delayed effect of too many slices of Manchego cheese.

She recommends you skip this one.

Dear Editor,

There is a tragedy looming in our midst.  It is time we face the problems that the past generations have perpetuated and ignored and come face to face with the demons of our own design.  This issue is undoubtedly the defining problem of our generation and our commitment to its solution will determine whether this civilization is remembered for its greatness or lost to the obscurity of time.

I am, of course, referring to the treatment of our feet.

Why, you ask, might a pair of hands be so concerned with the day to day struggles of such a lowly class of appendages? Hands have always asserted their superiority.  We capture the beauty of nature in a brush stroke, bring heaven to earth with our sculpture, reside far above the dirty muck of the city streets… we have opposable thumbs.

Yet the time has come to embrace our smelly brethren.  We must realize that their treatment by the people will one day become our treatment.  Our futures are linked just as a mani/pedi combo is always a better deal than making separate appointments.

Just yesterday I heard an alarming message from my own department of FEET_CaN (Furthering Equality Everywhere for Toes_Campaign against Neglect).  It’s touching words and poetic speech about the trials and tribulations of a simple pair of feet deceived by the misnomer “vacation” into committing to hours of walking and hiking stirred something in my knuckles.  I faithfully reproduce it now.

First, I want to apologize for the messy note.  No one ever teaches a toe how to hold a pencil. In fact, toes don’t get much of anything.  I’m just a simple bit of bone, skin, muscle, blood vessels and nerve endings, but hey, I can produce my own calcium sheeting. Amazes me every time.  It’s so great to have someone share your feelings with and talk to. None of the other toes are that social.  I remember how excited I was to first learn that my stem cell would join the proud ranks of toe cells…

(Next seven pages omitted:  Excuse us as we jump to the relevant parts.)

…of course as a teenager my first love was M. Sockie and the Lint Dawg Crew.  How they could rock.  But it never would have worked out.  M. Sockie had trust issues with the number of socks I was seeing and I was expected to settle down with the toe next door…

(Sorry, I thought that was the end of it.)

…but no one knows the pain of losing so many friends.  One minute you’re playing the Alphabet name game with all of these brand new skin cells and the next they just give up on life and callus up.  Sloughed away like any old epidermis…

(Almost there.)

…so I said, “no, the lime goes IN the coconut…”

(In summary the toe then proceeded to described in length its battles with the meaning of life and its place in the universe, a nasty stint with polish fumes and how it turned its life around with the help of Bruno Mars special edition OPI in “You’re Amazing Just the Way You Are: Perfectly Pink”)

…and thats how one brave toe nail clipping stopped global warming and saved the polar bears. Or maybe that was a different story…

Anyway, everyone at FEET_CaN feel that enough is enough.  We seek to publicize the injustices against us in hopes of garnering support.  We will now share our sad story.

Today, all members of the toe and greater foot region faced the biggest insult since we were convicted in the Achilles‘ manslaughter case  (evidence about the “supposed” weakness of Achilles’ heel was circumstantial at best).   We are a people of podiatry pride, and we take complaints about our craft very seriously.  We’ve bravely completed all of our missions: get to the top of the Alps ridge in Pontresina, carry everyone through six hours of walking in Vienna, and we even hiked to the top of the Prague Castle hill twice when everyone got lost.  But then we’re walking around today and everyone starts complaining: there’s a blister, we shouldn’t be tired so quickly, and saying we’re tripping over ourselves.  But we were facing major political instability from regional uprisings.  First the feet on Evan teased our left foot for being a quarter of a size smaller than the right.  Our right foot exacerbated the situation by saying the left foot was adopted and then the left foot started threatening to leave in search of a “true” size 7 1/2.  (Shoe size is a touchy subject.)

So I’ll admit, I started getting a little cranky.  It’s hard enough trying to keep all of the other toes in line and now everyone is blaming the Foot Association for not working hard enough.  I mean none of our allies in the body are backing us up for all the work we did over the last two weeks and now to add insult to injury we start taking escalators.  Escalators! The ultimate in foot-duty failure. It was so embarrassing.

Walking through the vastness of Berlin was supposed to be a day of duty and pride and instead we feel disgrace and failure.  All we want is a little respect.  It’s hard work being a foot, but no one seems to care.  We know we’re way down on the body, but we have feelings too.  Stop calling us “tootsies” “dogs” or “boats.”  We do not have big hair, bark, or float.  The national body monetary deficit is not our fault.  You all are the ones that keep telling us to pay the bill.  And we’re sorry if you’d rather have paws or hooves.  Take it up with evolution.

In conclusion, the citizens of the Foot Association demand fairness, equality, respect, and a new pair of socks.  We like the ones with the heel cushion and breathable mesh.

Here at the Hand Association, all we can do is applaud such power, clarity, and sock expertise.  The future of body harmony is a place where we are not judged for our propensity for fungal infections but by our contribution to the common good. I call all of you to heed these brave words and remember your own feet and their daily struggles for acceptance and the strength to walk around Europe.  I leave you with this profound thought:

Now Upside Down Land needs no shoes

For the people’s feet took a cruise

The people feel dumb

Scooting around on their bum

So care for your feet, or you lose!

For feet everywhere!

Sincerely,

the Hand Association’s Regional Headquarters at Hannah

On behalf of all hands, related hand entities, and those residing in the immediate vicinity of the hands under jurisdiction of the Upper Body Unity Pact, we would like to offer an apology to the readers of the above-printed letter.  It was completely worthless, intellectually insulting, and a waste of your time.  The interns of fingers 1,4, and 7 wanted to try writing a political manifesto and printed this editorial without permission.  They have been fired.  We have formed a bi-part-of-the-body-san investigation committee into the mental health dangers of extended confinement in tennis shoes and will be publishing an official report eventually.  But probably not.  No one really cares about feet.
