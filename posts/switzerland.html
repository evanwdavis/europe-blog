Hannah · Switzerland

Gilligan's Island, Swiss Edition

by Hannah

Just sit right back and you'll hear a tale,

A tale of a fateful trip

That started from this tropic port

Aboard this tiny ship.

The mate was a mighty sailing man,

The skipper brave and sure.

Five passengers set sail that day

For a three hour tour, a three hour tour.


The weather started getting rough,

The tiny ship was tossed,

If not for the courage of the fearless crew

The minnow would be lost, the minnow would be lost.


The ship set ground on the shore of this uncharted desert isle

With Gilligan

The Skipper too,

The millionaire and his wife,

The movie star

The professor and Mary Ann,

Here on Gilligan's Isle.


So this is the tale of the castaways,

They're here for a long, long time,

They'll have to make the best of things,

It's an uphill climb.

Just sit right back and you'll hear a tale,

A tale of a fateful stay

That started on a famous train

One grey and gloomy day


The Bernina Express awaited us

Through the Alps we would go

Our bags were stowed, our cameras out

The wheels groaned below, the wheels groaned below


In wonder of the mountain peaks

We never even feared,

But once we crossed that country line,

Our money disappeared, our money disappeared


We stopped in a quiet town

with a budget reputation

Affordable,

Our guide book said,

Appeared a lot... for one night,

Got off the train,

The 13th of June, wallets doomed

To survive just one day


So this is the tale of what we learned

We hope it'll help you too

That when you go to Switzerland

Your money says Adieu


The first mate and the Skipper too,

Will do their very best,

To make the others comfortable,

In the tropic island nest.


No phone, no lights no motor cars,

Not a single luxury,

Like Robinson Crusoe,

As primitive as can be.


So join us here each week my friends,

You're sure to get a smile,

From seven stranded castaways,

Here on "Gilligan's Isle."


And walking dazed and much confused,

you'll puzzle at your map

In this strange, new world you'll even pay

For water from the tap

(not kidding... $3USD)


It truly is an amazing place

But don't eat, sleep, or freeze

If you can live on scenery,

Say Pontresina, please!


We'll probably never make it home,

So our warning don't ignore,

That Swiss Francs, known as CHF

Means: "Charge higher for..."
